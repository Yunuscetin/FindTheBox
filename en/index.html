Multiplayer browser game

Create a room, invite your friends, and be the first to find the hidden green box.

Find the Green Box is a room-based multiplayer browser game built for desktop and mobile. Players join with a room code or invite link, open boxes in turns, and the player who finds the green box wins the round and gains an advantage in the next round.
