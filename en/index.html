Score-based tournament

Create a room, invite your friends, collect green boxes each round, and finish on top of the final score table.

Find the Green Box is a room-based multiplayer browser game built for desktop and mobile. Each round contains multiple green boxes. Players open boxes in turn, and every green box adds to the player's score.
