Room-based party game

Share a room link, join instantly, and compete to collect the most green boxes by the end of the tournament.

Find the Green Box is a fast multiplayer browser game for friend groups. No installation is needed. Players join the same room, open boxes in turn, and every green box they uncover adds directly to their final score.

Group size 2-8 players One room code for the whole lobby
Win condition Highest score Collect more greens than everyone else
