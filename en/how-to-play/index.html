Step-by-step guide

Create a room, invite players, open boxes in turns, and collect every green box you can.

Find the Green Box is a room-code multiplayer browser game. The goal is no longer to produce a single round winner, but to finish the full 5-round tournament with the highest total green-box score.

Game steps 5 steps
  • 1. Create a roomChoose 2 to 8 players
  • 2. Share the linkFriends join with a link or room code
  • 3. Start the tournamentThe host begins the game
  • 4. Open boxesPlayers take turns making picks
  • 5. Finish on topThe highest total green-box score wins
Round system

The tournament has 5 rounds with 100, 80, 60, 50, and 40 boxes. The green-box counts are 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1. Every turn follows the normal one-pick order.
